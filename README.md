# A Vegan Recipe Collection
**Digital Asset Management, Belmont University**  
**Kayla Ahrndt**
04/2025

This is the first data science project I've ever made that is directly and selfishly beneficial to me!
When thinking about what kinds of collections would be helpful to me, I found myself thinking about post-grad life and the problems I knew I'll be facing.
For instance, I love to cook and eat in for financial, hobby, and human reasons! But while surfing the web on recipe blogs, whether exclusively vegan or not, 
there's often much that I have to scroll through to find the things that matter to me most:


- RECIPE NAME
- INGREDIENTS
- COOKING TIME (MINUTES)
- ENERGY/DIFFICULTY (1 - 5)
- COST ESTIMATE ($ - $$$)
- MEAL TYPE
- PRIMARY NUTRIENTS
- SOURCE
- IMAGE																	


The previously mentioned searching takes an unpredictable amount of time and energy, and something that meets the most important condition(s) for me isn't necessarily even something that I would prefer to be cooking or eating.
So, I created a collection of recipies that either are or would be in my comfort and enjoyment to make, where key words can be used to find what I'm looking for.


RECIPE NAME		
- Recipe name listed on website posting
  
INGREDIENTS
- List format with 3 spaces between ingredients
  
COOKING TIME (MINUTES)
- Prep + cooking time in minutes
  
ENERGY/DIFFICULTY (1 - 5)
- 1-5 scale of the amount of difficulty or energy it may take to make, as determined by the author while reading the instructions
  
COST ESTIMATE ($ - $$$)
- $ - $$$ USD scale based on recipe author or site author's estimate
  
MEAL TYPE
- List format with 3 spaces between meal types
  - 'meal', 'meal prep', 'side', 'gf', 'breakfast', 'dessert', 'soup', 'pasta', 'special occasion'
    
PRIMARY NUTRIENTS
- List format with 3 spaces between primary nutrients
  - 'protien', 'vegetable', 'carb', 'fruit'
    
SOURCE
- Hyperlink to recipe posting
  
IMAGE
- Hyperlink to recipe image from recipe posting
